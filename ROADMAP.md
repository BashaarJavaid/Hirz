# Roadmap

The build order for Hirz, sequenced so there is something demoable at the end of every phase. Kept as a living checklist: update this file as items complete rather than letting it drift from reality. Items are complete only when their `verify:` check passes and, where a `THREAT_MODEL.md` row is involved, the row is earned by code.

**Hackathon dates (Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026):**

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| AWS promotional credit request deadline | 2026-10-21 12:00 PT |
| **Submission deadline** | **2026-10-23 12:00 PT** |
| Judging period | 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-20 |
| Winners announced | on or about 2026-12-03 |

Hirz is designed as a startup; the hackathon is its first milestone. Phases 0–8 are the hackathon build with a **cut line** after Phase 8. Phase 9 onward is the company. See [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](./ARCHITECTURE.md) for what each item means and [`docs/adr/`](./docs/adr/) for the reasoning behind deferrals.

## Phase 9 onward — the company (documented, not built)

Each item has a trigger so it is built when needed, not speculatively.

- **Physical-Echo demo.** Trigger: access to an Alexa+ device. The community Skill bridge is already used for one read-only console clip (item 41a); on hardware it would still lack account linking and cards. ADR-007.
- **Read and disclosure rules.** Trigger: a household asks to limit what Hirz says aloud, or what another home may be told. A constitution domain for reads ("tell Malik when I ask for help; don't share my daily activity"). Today the fixed rules are in code: no channel values or verification history are ever spoken, and nothing crosses between homes except what the resident's own rules send.
- **An approval page the worker does not serve.** Trigger: item 38d is built. It moves the compromised-worker row from Partial toward Yes by serving the passkey approval screen from static hosting the worker cannot change.
- **Caregiver view across homes.** Trigger: the first customer who manages a parent's home. One login, several households, each with its own constitution and the resident's consent; today the link between homes is a trusted contact.

Doorbell and camera motion events as a "quiet morning" signal, governed by a rule the parent writes about what the caregiver may be told and never shares video. A new domain, deliberately not in the hackathon build.
- **Trademark clearance for the name.** Trigger: before incorporation or any paid launch. The project was renamed on 2026-09-17 after collisions in its own category, and the author's own USPTO search for the new name in classes 9 and 42 came back clear that day; a formal clearance (an attorney's search, and other jurisdictions) is still owed.

- **Two-person activation for constitution changes.** Trigger: a household asks for it. An approval workflow overlay on activation, same pattern as approvals.
